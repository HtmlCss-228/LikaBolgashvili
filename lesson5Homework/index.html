Planets

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It features the Great Red Spot, a giant storm, and has over 79 moons, including Ganymede, the largest moon in the Solar System. Jupiter's magnetic field is the strongest in the Solar System.

Saturn, famous for its stunning rings, is the second-largest planet. Its rings are made of ice and rock. Saturn's moon, Titan, has lakes of liquid methane and a thick atmosphere, making it a fascinating target for exploration.

Mars, known as the Red Planet, gets its color from iron oxide in its soil. It has a thin atmosphere and contains Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the Solar System. Scientists believe Mars once had liquid water, possibly supporting life in the past.
